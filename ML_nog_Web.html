About Moonlight Foundation

Moonlight Foundation was established on july 24, 2025 with a simple yet powerful mission: to ensure no child goes to bed hungry. We believe that access to nutritious food is a basic human right and work tirelessly to provide meals to street children and families in need.

Our approach combines immediate hunger relief with long-term sustainable solutions, creating a positive impact on both people and the planet.

Sustainability Efforts

At Moonlight Foundation, we're committed to protecting our planet while feeding those in need. Our sustainability initiatives include:

  • Local Produce Sourcing

    We partner with local farmers to source fresh, seasonal ingredients, reducing food miles and supporting our local economy.

  • Food Waste Reduction

    We implement efficient meal planning and distribution systems to minimize food waste throughout our operations.

  • Biodegradable Packaging

    All our meals are served in eco-friendly, biodegradable packaging to reduce environmental impact.
